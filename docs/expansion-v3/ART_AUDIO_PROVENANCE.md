# Audio provenance

The wind, low hum, pad and sparse upper tone are generated by code in `MarsOutpostAudio.cs` at runtime. They are original mathematical oscillator combinations: deterministic, weighted harmonics from roughly 200–730 Hz for the wind; a low sinusoid for the hum; and a slowly modulated chord and upper tone for the music. Frequencies and loop lengths use whole cycles to make the wrap continuous. Existing notification tones are synthesized in the same code. No recording, downloaded loop, song, or external music asset is used. This is a record of the production method, not a legal certification.
